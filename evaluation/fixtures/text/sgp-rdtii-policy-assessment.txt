RDTII 2.1 Singapore economy assessment excerpt

Infrastructure requirements: Singapore does not implement a local data centre, local server, or other physical infrastructure requirement.

Binding data-transfer commitments: Singapore has joined trade agreements with binding commitments to cross-border transfer and free flow of data, including the CPTPP and RCEP.

Cybersecurity framework: Singapore has a dedicated horizontal cybersecurity legal framework under the Cybersecurity Act 2018.
