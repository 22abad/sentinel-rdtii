Introduction: De minimis refers to the minimum value of the goods below which no duties and taxes are being collected by the Customs (see also Transitional Standard 4.13 of the WCO Revised Kyoto 
Convention and Art. 7.8.2 (d) of the WTO TFA). According to the WCO Immediate Release Guidelines, the de minimis value should be the intrinsic value of the goods (f.o.b.), i.e. the invoice value of the 
goods without transport charges.
