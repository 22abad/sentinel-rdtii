[ ] ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows: Short title and commencement 1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Sedition (Amendment) Act 2015. (2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette. Amendment of section 2 2. The Sedition Act 1948 [Act 15], which is referred to as the “principal Act” in this Act, is amended in section 2 by inserting before the definition of “Government” the following definition: ‘ “by electronic means”, in relation to a publication, means— (a) broadcasted so as to be available for reception by members of the public or of a section of the public; or (b) communicated through network services to members of the public or of a section of the public;’. Table of contents 2. 3 This section aims to amend the definition of "publication" in section 2 of the Sedition Act 1948 to include publication by electronic means (which includes broadcast and communication through network services). 3 3. 4 "This section aims to amend the definition of ""seditious tendency"" in section 3 of the Sedition Act 1948. It removes criticism of government and judiciary from the definition of seditious tendency. However, the definition now includes the promotion of :""ill will, hostility or hatred between persons or groups of persons on the ground of religion"". 4 4. 5 This section increases the punishment for sedition offences under section 4(1) of the Sedition Act 1948.
