Consumer Protection (Electronic Trade Transaction) Regulations 2024 [P.U. (A) 449/2024] — regulations 3, 4 and 5 (online marketplace disclosure duties)

Disclosure of information
3.     (1)     Any online marketplace supplier shall disclose the information specified
in the Schedule on the online marketplace.
(2)     For the purposes of subregulation (1), any online marketplace supplier
shall not disclose or provide any information as may be specified in the Schedule
that he knows or has reason to believe is false or misleading.
Language
4.     (1)     The information disclosed by the online marketplace supplier on
the online marketplace shall be in the national language.
(2)     In addition to the national language, any other languages may be used
for the disclosure of information by the online marketplace supplier on the online
marketplace.
Costs and additional services
5.     Any online marketplace supplier who has agreed to receive payment as
a consideration for the supply of goods or services offered or supplied shall—
(a)     bear the cost of re-delivery to the purchaser if the goods received by
the purchaser is materially different or contained defects; or
(b)     provide services which are reasonably fit or as offered or supplied.
