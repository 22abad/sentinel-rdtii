Identity Verification Services Act 2023 (No. 115 of 2023) — section 10 (extra privacy obligations: destruction of facial images)

To take reasonable steps to destroy each facial image of an individual that is created, for the purposes of the request, by the party requesting the service, as soon as reasonably practicable after the image is no longer required for the purposes of the request, unless the image is: (i) a Commonwealth record; or (ii) required by a law of the Commonwealth, a State or a Territory, or by an order of a court or tribunal, to be retained.
