Healthcare Services (General) Regulations 2021 (S 1035/2021), subsidiary to the Healthcare Services Act 2020 — regulation 37

A licensee must keep and maintain, for such period and in such manner as the Director-General may specify, an accurate, complete and up-to-date patient health record of every patient in accordance with this regulation. (2) A patient health record must contain all of the following information relating to the patient: (a) name; (b) identification number or passport number; (c) gender; (d) date of birth.
